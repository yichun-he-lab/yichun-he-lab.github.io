# Research
We develop artificial intelligence and machine learning methods to build cell intelligence and advance our understanding of biological systems across multiple scales.

Our research is motivated by the rapid development of new biological technologies that measure diverse molecular modalities at unprecedented throughput and resolution. We aim to explore complex multi-scale biological systems from molecular signatures, cell states, to tissue architectures, functions and behaviors, and understand how these systems change under perturbations, diseases, and treatments.

We address these challenges by **developing AI methods that can extract meaningful biological insights from complex, high-dimensional, and multi-modal biological data**. Our research involves developing both new **mathematical theory** and **practical algorithms** that draw on techniques from deep learning, causal inference, multimodal learning, and computational neuroscience.

### Spatial Multi-omics and Cell Intelligence
<table>
<tr>
<td width="70%" markdown="1">
We develop AI methods to characterize spatially resolved cell types and molecular profiles at scale, bridging cellular features with spatial contexts across various technologies. Our goal is to **map the molecular landscape of biological tissues** and understand how spatial organization drives cellular function.

_Key words: spatial transcriptomics, spatial multi-omics, cell type mapping, molecular atlases, deep learning, neural fields_

* **Spatial molecular atlases**: comprehensive mapping of cell types and molecular profiles across entire organs.
    * [**Spatial Atlas of the Mouse Central Nervous System** (Nature 2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06569-5): first molecular-resolution atlas of the entire mouse brain.
    * [**FuseMap** (bioRxiv 2024)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.05.27.594872v1): universal spatial molecular atlas framework.
* **Spatial analysis tools**: computational methods for analyzing spatial gene expression patterns.
    * [**ClusterMap** (Nature Communications 2021)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-26044-x): multi-scale clustering analysis of spatial gene expression.
    * [**TempoMap** (Nature Methods 2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-023-01829-8): spatiotemporally resolved transcriptomics for RNA kinetic landscapes.

### Brain-Machine Interfaces and NeuroAI
<table>
<tr>
<td width="70%" markdown="1">
We develop AI methods for decoding dynamic biological processes in real-time, with a focus on brain-machine interfaces and long-term neural activity analysis. Our goal is to **understand brain mechanisms and enable more intelligent Bio-AI systems**.

_Key words: brain-machine interfaces, neural decoding, real-time AI, bioelectronics, computational neuroscience_

* **Real-time neural decoding**: AI methods for decoding months-long neural activity from the same cells.
    * [**AutoSort** (bioRxiv 2024)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.10.14.618046v1): end-to-end multimodal deep learning for real-time neural activity decoding.
* **Bio-inspired AI systems**: soft bioelectronics and brain-computer interfaces.
    * [**Brain implantation via embryonic development** (Nature 2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09106-8): tissue-level-soft bioelectronics for brain interfaces.
    * [**FlexiSoft** (bioRxiv 2025)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.29.651325v1): plastic-elastomer heterostructure for robust flexible brain-computer interfaces.

### Multimodal AI and Single-Cell Multi-omics
<table>
<tr>
<td width="70%" markdown="1">
We develop multimodal AI methods to integrate diverse biological data and understand cell states and dynamics. Our goal is to **create unified frameworks for analyzing complex, multi-modal biological datasets**.

_Key words: multimodal learning, single-cell multi-omics, data integration, interpretable AI, causal inference_

* **Multimodal data integration**: AI methods for integrating diverse biological data sources.
    * [**UnitedNet** (Nature Communications 2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-37477-x): multi-task learning for single-cell multi-modality biology.
    * [**Electro-seq** (Cell 2023)](https://www.cell.com/cell/abstract/S0092-8674(23)00286-6): multimodal charting of molecular and functional cell states.
* **Causal and interpretable AI**: methods for extracting biological insights from complex models.
    * [**Organoid symmetry breaking** (Cell 2023)](https://www.cell.com/cell/abstract/S0092-8674(22)01586-0): controlling organoid symmetry breaking to uncover excitable systems.

### Agentic AI for Scientific Discovery
<table>
<tr>
<td width="70%" markdown="1">
We develop autonomous AI agents for experimental planning, data analysis, and scientific discovery in biology. Our goal is to **enable autonomous scientific discovery** through intelligent AI systems.

_Key words: agentic AI, autonomous experimentation, scientific discovery, spatial biology, AI agents_

* **Spatial biology agents**: autonomous AI systems for spatial biology research.
    * [**SpatialAgent** (bioRxiv 2025)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.03.646459v1): autonomous AI agent for spatial biology.
    * [**STAgent** (bioRxiv 2025)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.01.646731v1): spatial transcriptomics AI agent for hPSC-pancreas maturation.
    * [**SpikeAgent** (bioRxiv 2025)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.02.11.637754v1): spike sorting AI agent.
* **AI-driven design**: methods for AI-guided biological design and discovery.
    * [**DRAKES** (NeurIPS 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13643): fine-tuning discrete diffusion models for DNA and protein design.
