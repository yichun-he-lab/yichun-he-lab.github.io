Resources

Software and data resources developed by Yichun He with collaborators. Explore the repositories and portals to use them in your own research.

SCIENTIFIC AGENT↗

SpatialAgent

AI agents that connect biological questions with spatial analysis tools, literature, and data.

SPATIAL FOUNDATION MODEL↗

FuseMap

Integrate spatial transcriptomic atlases and learn a shared representation of genes, cells, and tissues.

INTERACTIVE DATA PORTAL↗

molCCF

Explore a molecular common coordinate framework spanning 18.6 million cells and 26,665 genes.

SPATIAL ANALYSIS↗

ClusterMap

Identify cells and tissue structures directly from the spatial distribution of RNA molecules.

MULTIMODAL LEARNING↗

UnitedNet

Integrate biological modalities, predict missing measurements, and explore interpretable relationships.

NEURAL DECODING↗

AutoSort

Decode neural signals using multimodal information for long-term recordings from the same cells.

ATLAS & ANALYSIS CODE↗

mCNS atlas

Explore the spatial atlas of the mouse central nervous system at molecular resolution.
