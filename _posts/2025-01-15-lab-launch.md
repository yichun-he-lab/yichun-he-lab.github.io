---
title: "He Lab Launches at UIUC"
date: 2025-01-15
---

We are excited to announce the launch of the He Lab at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign! 

The lab will focus on developing artificial intelligence and machine learning methods to build cell intelligence and advance our understanding of biological systems across multiple scales. Our research spans spatial multi-omics, brain-machine interfaces, agentic AI for scientific discovery, and interpretable machine learning for biology.

We are actively recruiting motivated students and postdoctoral researchers. Please visit our [Join](/join) page for more information about opportunities in the lab.
