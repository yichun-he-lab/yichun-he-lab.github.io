# Welcome to the He Lab!
{: .display-5}
<br>

{: .welcomefont}
We are a <b>computational biology and AI</b> research group in the [Siebel School of Computing and Data Science](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/).

{: .welcomefont}
We develop <b>artificial intelligence</b> and <b>machine learning</b> methods to build cell intelligence and advance our understanding of biological systems across multiple scales.

{: .welcomefont}
Current research interests include spatial multi-omics, brain-machine interfaces, agentic AI for scientific discovery, and interpretable machine learning for biology.
