# Welcome to the He Lab!
{: .display-5}
<br>

{: .welcomefont}
We are a <b>computational biology and AI</b> research group in the [Siebel School of Computing and Data Science](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/).

{: .welcomefont}
We develop <b>artificial intelligence</b> and <b>machine learning</b> methods to build cell intelligence and advance our understanding of biological systems across multiple scales.

{: .welcomefont}
Current research interests include spatial multi-omics, brain-machine interfaces, agentic AI for scientific discovery, and interpretable machine learning for biology.

## Yichun He
**Incoming Assistant Professor of Computer Science**

I am an incoming Assistant Professor in the [Siebel School of Computing and Data Science](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) starting 2027. Currently, I am an [Eric and Wendy Schmidt Fellow](https://www.ericandwendyschmidtcenter.org/) at the [Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard](https://www.broadinstitute.org/). I received my Ph.D. in the [School of Engineering and Applied Sciences at Harvard University](https://seas.harvard.edu/). I received my bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering at the University of Science and Technology of China with the highest honor Guomoruo Scholarship.

My research aims to build cell intelligence by exploring complex multi-scale biological systems from molecular signatures, cell states, to tissue architectures, functions and behaviors, and how the system changes under perturbations, diseases, and treatments with AI. I use causal machine learning to identify the underlying mechanisms and principles of biological systems, multimodal machine learning to integrate data from various sources, and interpretable machine learning to extract biological insights from complex models and data. I also design bio-inspired AI frameworks with the goal of advancing our understanding of brain mechanisms and enabling more intelligent Bio-AI systems.
