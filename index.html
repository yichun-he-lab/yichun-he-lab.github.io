He Lab

Building Cell Intelligence through AI and Computational Innovation

We are passionate about accelerating discovery in biology and health through advanced AI/ML approaches. Our research spans spatial biology, multi-modal data integration, autonomous scientific discovery, and computational neuroscience to understand complex biological systems.

Principal Investigator: Yichun He

Current Affiliation: Eric and Wendy Schmidt Fellow, Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard

Future Home: Assistant Professor, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (Starting 2027)

Research

We are passionate about accelerating discovery in biology and health through advanced AI/ML approaches. Our work spans multiple directions:

1

Mapping Spatially Resolved Cell Types and Molecular Profiles at Scale

Characterize whole-brain spatially resolved cell types at molecular resolution with scalable AI and spatial transcriptomics.

2

Linking Cellular Characteristics to Spatial Contexts Across Technologies

Bridge cellular features with spatial contexts across various technologies and conditions with contextual AI.

3

Uncovering Long-Term Cell Functions and Behaviors

Decode dynamic biological processes in long term with real-time AI and brain computer interfaces.

4

Integrating Diverse Biological Data to Understand Cell States and Dynamics

Integrate diverse biological data with multi-modal AI and single-cell multi-omics.

5

Enabling Autonomous Experimentation, Analysis, and Scientific Discovery

Autonomous experimental planning, data analysis, and scientific discovery in spatial biology with Agentic AI.

Yichun He

I am an incoming assistant professor at UIUC CS starting 2027. Currently I am an Eric and Wendy Schmidt Fellow at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard. I received my Ph.D in the School of Engineering and Applied Sciences at Harvard University. I received bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering at the University of Science and Technology of China with the highest honor Guomoruo Scholarship.

My research aims to build cell intelligence:

  1. I explore the complex multi-scale biological systems from molecular signatures, cell states, to tissue architectures, functions and behaviors and how the system changes under perturbations, diseases, and treatments with AI.
  2. I design bio-inspired AI frameworks, with the goal of advancing our understanding of brain mechanisms and enable more intelligent Bio-AI systems.
